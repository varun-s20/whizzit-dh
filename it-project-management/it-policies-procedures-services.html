The problem

Good technology still needs clear rules

Technology is only as effective as the processes behind it. When documentation is missing, staff create informal workarounds, and those inconsistencies gradually increase security risk and make everything harder to manage.

We help businesses across the UK replace guesswork with clear, practical policies, so people know what’s expected, and technology is managed consistently across every team.

Policies are written to fit how your business genuinely operates, so they hold up under audit rather than sitting unread in a folder. WhizzIT is Cyber Essentials certified and a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider, so our security fundamentals are independently assessed rather than simply claimed. It is priced per user at a fixed monthly cost rather than bundled into rigid packages, so it scales with your team. Everything runs through the same UK service desk that handles your day-to-day tickets, so there is no separate team to chase.

Common risks we find

  • User onboarding processes varying between departments
  • Former employees retaining unnecessary system access
  • Password and authentication standards applied inconsistently
  • Undocumented remote working arrangements
  • Important IT procedures relying on verbal instructions
  • Unclear assignment of security responsibilities

Case study

Standardising IT processes across a growing multi-site organisation

The problem: a professional services business expanding into multiple offices found different teams handling routine IT tasks in their own way. Onboarding, software approvals, password management and device allocation all varied between locations.

Our action: we reviewed existing workflows, consulted department managers and produced practical documentation covering user lifecycle management, acceptable use, password standards, remote working and device management.
