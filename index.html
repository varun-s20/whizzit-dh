Frequently asked questions

What is a managed IT services provider (MSP)?

An MSP is an outsourced IT partner that proactively monitors, manages and secures your business technology. That covers IT support, cloud services, cyber security and disaster recovery, without the need for a full in-house IT team.

Why use an MSP instead of an in-house IT team?

For a similar budget to a single hire, an MSP gives you a whole team with a broad skill set, proactive monitoring and expert cyber security, all for a fixed monthly cost that scales with your business.

What types of businesses benefit from managed IT?

Any business that relies on technology. We support professional services, hospitality, healthcare, construction and more, keeping systems secure, compliant and productive.

How much does managed IT support cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your business and the level of support you need. We offer flexible support plans with transparent, predictable monthly fees.

Brands we partner with

We hold partner status with the vendors behind the tools we deploy, so your licensing, support and escalation come direct from the source rather than through a reseller chain.
