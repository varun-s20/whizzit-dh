A smooth move from Google to Microsoft 365

How WhizzIT migrated Ixia Clinical from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365, moving 10 users and more than 1TB of email and files across without loss or downtime to clinical work.

Case study

How the project ran

01

What they needed

Ixia Clinical needed to migrate from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365 while maintaining data integrity and avoiding any disruption to clinical operations. All email, documents, shared drives and meetings had to transfer without loss or downtime.

02

What we did

WhizzIT managed the complete migration: preparing the Microsoft 365 environment, transferring all 10 users and 1TB+ of email and files, and sequencing the switchover to prevent disruption. We validated the successful arrival of mail, files and calendars before retiring the legacy platform, with ongoing IT support in place once the move was done.

03

Where they are now

The business moved onto Microsoft 365 smoothly, email, files and calendars all arrived intact, and now has professional IT support available on demand whenever the team needs it.
