What it covers

Defence in depth for your business

We protect businesses from digital threats through a comprehensive approach that covers cloud platforms, local networks, email systems and user devices. We do this for businesses across London and Hertfordshire, and remotely for clients UK-wide.

Our strategy combines proactive threat detection, real-time monitoring and intelligent filtering to stop malware, phishing and unauthorised access before it reaches your team.
