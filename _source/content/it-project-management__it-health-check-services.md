### Technology does not need to fail before it needs reviewing

Most IT problems build up gradually, out of sight, long before anything actually breaks. Infrastructure quietly ages, security settings drift and backups run without ever being tested, until a single issue turns into an expensive one.
A regular health check gives you an honest understanding of your IT condition. We help businesses across the UK identify hidden risks, improve resilience and make informed technology decisions with confidence.
The health check assesses your environment against current good practice and returns a prioritised list of what to fix, in what order, and why. We are a team of five engineers supporting more than 30 UK businesses, from two-user teams to organisations of 150+ users. WhizzIT is Cyber Essentials certified and a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider, so our security fundamentals are independently assessed rather than simply claimed.
Common risks we find
- Infrastructure approaching end of life without a replacement plan
- Security settings not reviewed for several years
- Backup processes running but never tested
- Cloud services with inconsistent configurations
- User permissions not reflecting business requirements
- Performance issues developing gradually over time
