### AI is already being used across your business

Staff are already using artificial intelligence to draft emails, analyse data and summarise meetings, frequently before any formal governance exists. Adoption tends to happen quietly: different departments pick different tools, business data gets pasted into public platforms, and managers have little visibility into what’s being used.
The real challenge isn’t AI itself. It’s inconsistent adoption, undefined accountability and a lack of practical guidance. We help you introduce sensible oversight that balances innovation with information protection and management visibility.
Governance work sets out where AI tools may be used, what data may go near them and who signs that off, before shadow adoption sets the rules for you. Across 2025 our average first response time was 9 minutes and our average resolution time was 6 hours, measured across every ticket we handled. We are a team of five engineers supporting more than 30 UK businesses, from two-user teams to organisations of 150+ users.
Common risks we find
- Employees using public AI platforms without clear guidance
- Business information being entered into external AI tools
- Different departments following different AI practices
- No documented policy covering acceptable AI usage
- Limited visibility into AI tools used across the business
- Managers unsure how AI-generated content should be reviewed
