### What are managed cloud services?

Managed cloud services mean handing the day-to-day running of your cloud platforms, Microsoft 365, Azure, Google Workspace, to a provider who keeps them secure, available and cost-effective. You can see how we structure that on our
- [CTA] managed cloud services -> /managed-cloud-services/
- [CTA] page.
Rather than juggling licences, security settings and backups yourself, you get a partner who does it properly and proactively.

### What’s usually included

A good managed cloud service typically covers:
- User onboarding, offboarding and day-to-day changes
- Licence management and cost control
- Security and access configuration
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Monitoring and proactive maintenance
- Support for your team

### The benefits

Done well, managed cloud gives you stronger security, predictable costs and less time lost to admin. It also means expert help when you need to migrate, scale or solve a problem.
Crucially, it closes the gaps businesses often miss, like untested backups or over-provisioned licences quietly costing money.

### Is it right for you?

If your team relies on cloud tools every day, and managing them is either eating your time or being neglected, managed cloud is almost certainly worth it. The best way to know is a short review of your current setup.
That’s where we’d usually start: understand what you’ve got, then make it secure, reliable and cost-effective.
