### What shapes cloud projects for London businesses

Cloud migrations for London businesses tend to be constrained less by the technology than by the building, the lease and the calendar. The pattern repeats often enough to plan around.
- [CTA] 01Serviced offices remove control of the connection
In multi-tenant and serviced buildings you rarely control the incoming line, the contention ratio or the risers. That argues for cloud-first services, cellular failover and a design that does not assume the landlord’s network behaves.
- [CTA] 02Lease events are the real migration deadline
A move or a lease break is usually what forces the decision, which compresses the timeline and makes sequencing matter. Migrating before the move, rather than during it, avoids doing two difficult things in the same week.
- [CTA] 03Hybrid working is the default, not the exception
London teams split the week between office, home and client sites more than most. That pushes identity, conditional access and device management to the front of the design rather than treating them as a later phase.
