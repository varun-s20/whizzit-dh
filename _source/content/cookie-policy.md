### What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to improve performance, and to provide information to the site owner about how the site is used.

### The cookies we use


#### Essential cookies

These are necessary for the website to function correctly, for example remembering your cookie preferences. They cannot be switched off in our systems.

#### Analytics cookies

We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website, so we can measure and improve performance. These help us know which pages are the most and least popular.

#### Session recording & heatmaps

We use Microsoft Clarity to better understand how visitors use our site through behavioural metrics, heatmaps and session replay. Clarity sets cookies and captures how you navigate and interact with our pages, such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, which we use to diagnose issues and improve the site. It loads only after you accept cookies on our consent banner. See the
- [CTA] Microsoft Privacy Statement -> https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
- for how Microsoft processes this data.

#### Functional cookies

These enable enhanced functionality and personalisation, such as remembering choices you make.

### Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you refuse or accept cookies, delete existing cookies, and be notified when a new cookie is set. Please note that disabling some cookies may affect how the website functions.
- In Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, look under Settings → Privacy for cookie controls.
- To opt out of analytics across sites, you can use the tools provided by the relevant analytics provider.
