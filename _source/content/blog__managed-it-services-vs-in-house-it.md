### The in-house option

An internal team knows your business intimately and is always on hand. But hiring is expensive, one or two people can’t cover every discipline, and holidays, illness and turnover leave gaps.
For many small and medium businesses, a full in-house team is hard to justify and hard to keep.

### The managed option

A managed provider gives you a whole team, support, cloud, networking, security, for a predictable monthly fee, with proactive monitoring and no single point of failure.
The trade-off is that they’re not sitting in your office, though good providers feel like an extension of your team.

### The best of both

It’s not always either/or. Many businesses run a hybrid model: an internal person or team for hands-on and business-specific needs, with a managed provider handling monitoring, security, out-of-hours cover and specialist projects.
This co-managed approach combines local knowledge with broad expertise and resilience.

### How to decide

Weigh cost, the breadth of skills you need, how critical uptime is, and how much resilience matters. For most SMEs, managed or co-managed IT delivers more capability and less risk than going it alone.
