### The real problem usually appears after data has gone

Businesses depend on Microsoft 365 for email, collaboration and file storage, but cloud storage alone doesn’t guarantee full data protection. A deleted mailbox, a lost SharePoint file or a ransomware attack can halt operations when there’s no dedicated recovery strategy.
Microsoft 365’s native features were never designed to replace a dedicated backup. We close that gap with reliable backup, proactive monitoring and fast recovery, built around how your teams actually work.
Backup sits alongside your Microsoft 365 tenancy covering Exchange Online, SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams, with restores handled by us rather than left to you to work out. It is priced per user at a fixed monthly cost rather than bundled into rigid packages, so it scales with your team. Everything runs through the same UK service desk that handles your day-to-day tickets, so there is no separate team to chase.
Common data risks we uncover
- Reliance only on Microsoft’s native retention features
- Exchange Online mailboxes without dedicated backup
- SharePoint and Teams data that can’t be easily recovered
- Inconsistent recovery policies across departments
- Limited reporting on backup success and recovery readiness
- Business information exposed to accidental deletion or ransomware
