### Frequently asked questions

- [CTA] What is a managed IT services provider (MSP)?
- [CTA] An MSP is an outsourced IT partner that proactively monitors, manages and secures your business technology. That covers IT support, cloud services, cyber security and disaster recovery, without the need for a full in house IT team. -> /cyber-security/penetration-testing-services/
- [CTA] Why use an MSP instead of an in house IT team?
- [CTA] For a similar budget to a single hire, an MSP gives you a whole team with a broad skill set, proactive monitoring and expert cyber security, all for a fixed monthly cost that scales with your business. -> /cyber-security/password-management-services/
- [CTA] What types of businesses benefit from managed IT?
- [CTA] Any business that relies on technology. We support professional services, hospitality, healthcare, construction and more, keeping systems secure, compliant and productive. -> /cyber-security/network-security-services/
