### Good cyber security starts with the fundamentals

Cyber threats exploit simple weaknesses rather than sophisticated attacks. The controls that matter most are often the everyday ones, how accounts are set up, how devices are updated and how access is managed across your business.
We help organisations prepare for Cyber Essentials by reinforcing those foundational security controls and streamlining the certification process, so the standard becomes something your business genuinely meets rather than a box to tick.
We assess your environment against the standard first, tell you honestly how close you already are, then close the gaps and take you through certification. We are a team of five engineers supporting more than 30 UK businesses, from two-user teams to organisations of 150+ users. WhizzIT is Cyber Essentials certified and a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider, so our security fundamentals are independently assessed rather than simply claimed.
Common issues we identify
- User accounts with unnecessary administrator privileges
- Devices missing important security updates
- Multi-factor authentication applied inconsistently
- Unsupported software still connected to business systems
- Security policies not reflecting hybrid working arrangements
- Limited visibility into devices accessing company information

### Cyber Essentials FAQs

- [CTA] What is Cyber Essentials?
- [CTA] Cyber Essentials is a recognised, government-backed scheme that sets out the fundamental technical controls every business should have in place, covering areas such as firewalls, secure configuration, user access control, malware protection and security update management. The scheme is owned by the -> /cyber-security/security-awareness-training-services/
- [CTA] National Cyber Security Centre -> https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/cyberessentials/overview
- [CTA] , and there are two levels: Cyber Essentials, which is self-assessed and independently reviewed, and Cyber Essentials Plus, which adds hands-on technical verification of the same controls. -> /cyber-security/cyber-essentials-services/
- [CTA] How does WhizzIT help with certification?
- [CTA] We assess your environment against the requirements, close the gaps that would otherwise hold up assessment and guide you through the certification process, so you go in confident rather than hoping for the best. -> /cyber-security/configuration-review-services/
