### IT that scales with you

Startups grow in bursts. One month you’re five people, the next you’re twenty. Managed IT services flex with you: adding users, devices and services without big upfront investment or a hiring scramble. Our
- [CTA] IT support -> /it-support/
- [CTA] is designed to scale at that pace.
You get an IT capability that grows at your pace, not a fixed cost you have to grow into.

### Enterprise-grade security from day one

Investors, customers and partners increasingly expect solid security, and startups are attractive targets precisely because their defences are often thin. Managed IT builds in cyber security, backups and best practice from the start.
Getting this right early is far cheaper than retrofitting it after a scare or a failed due-diligence check.

### Focus on the business, not the tech

Founders and early teams should be building product and winning customers, not resetting passwords or fighting with email. Managed IT takes the technology off your plate so you can focus on what matters.
You get a whole team’s expertise, support, cloud, security, for far less than an early IT hire.

### Predictable costs

Cash is precious in a startup. Predictable monthly IT costs make budgeting easier and avoid the nasty surprises of break-fix billing.
