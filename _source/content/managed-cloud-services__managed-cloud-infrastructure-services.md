### Cloud infrastructure doesn’t usually fail overnight

Most organisations gradually add users and cloud services without evolving the infrastructure underneath them. Over time that leads to inconsistent performance, rising costs and security risks that stay hidden until something breaks.
Proactive design, monitoring and optimisation give you an environment that stays reliable, scalable and secure, so your infrastructure supports the business rather than holding it back.
Cloud infrastructure is monitored, patched and maintained continuously, with capacity and cost reviewed rather than left to drift. WhizzIT is Cyber Essentials certified and a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider, so our security fundamentals are independently assessed rather than simply claimed. It is priced per user at a fixed monthly cost rather than bundled into rigid packages, so it scales with your team. Everything runs through the same UK service desk that handles your day-to-day tickets, so there is no separate team to chase.
Common risks we uncover
- Environments struggling to scale with the business
- Single points of failure across critical systems
- Inconsistent configurations between services
- Limited visibility into resource utilisation
- Untested disaster recovery arrangements
- Performance bottlenecks affecting your users
