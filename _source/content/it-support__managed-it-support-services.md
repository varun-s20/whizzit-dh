### What proactive actually looks like

It is an easy word to say. Here is what it means in a normal month.

#### Monitoring that acts

Agents on your machines and servers report disk space, failing drives, missed backups, dropped services and security alerts. Thresholds create a ticket automatically, so the common ones get dealt with before anybody notices, rather than sitting on a dashboard nobody has open.

#### Patching on a schedule

Operating systems and third-party applications are updated on a defined cadence rather than whenever a user finally clicks the reminder. Unpatched software is still the most common way in, and patch management is one of the five Cyber Essentials controls for exactly that reason.

#### Assets and licences reviewed

We keep a live picture of what you own, how old it is and what it costs. That is how you avoid the two classic wastes: paying for subscriptions nobody uses, and running critical work on a machine that should have been replaced two years ago.

### What happens when you raise something

Every request lands in the same place and gets graded P1 to P5. A P1 is reserved for something that stops the business, like a total outage or a dead server, and gets immediate attention. Lower priorities still carry a committed response time, so nothing quietly rots at the bottom of a queue.
