### Skipping backups (or never testing them)

Plenty of businesses have backups they’ve never tested, and discover too late that they don’t work. Independent, regularly tested backups are non-negotiable. Testing them on a schedule is part of every
- [CTA] managed IT support -> /it-support/managed-it-support-services/

### Treating security as optional

Assuming you’re too small to be a target is a costly mistake. Basic controls, MFA, endpoint protection, staff awareness, prevent the majority of incidents and are well within reach.

### Relying on break-fix

Only calling for help once something breaks means more downtime and unpredictable bills. Proactive, managed support prevents problems and smooths costs.

### Neglecting updates

- [CTA] Unpatched systems -> https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/device-security-guidance
are one of the easiest ways in for attackers. Automated update and patch management closes those doors without relying on anyone to remember.

### No plan

Reactive, ad-hoc IT leads to wasted spend and nasty surprises. Even a simple roadmap aligns technology with where the business is going.
Avoiding these five mistakes puts most small businesses ahead of the pack, and a good managed partner handles all of them for you.
