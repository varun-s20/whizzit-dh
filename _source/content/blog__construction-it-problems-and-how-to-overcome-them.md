### Connectivity on site

Construction sites rarely come with reliable, business-grade internet. Teams need access to drawings, project management tools and email from locations that change constantly. We go into how we handle this on our
- [CTA] IT support for construction -> /it-support-construction/
- [CTA] page.
The fix is designing connectivity deliberately, 4G/5G routers, well-configured VPNs and cloud tools that tolerate patchy connections, rather than hoping site Wi-Fi will cope.

### Protecting project data

Drawings, models and contracts are the lifeblood of a project, and they’re increasingly targeted. Losing them to a failed laptop or ransomware can derail a programme.
Centralised, backed-up cloud storage with proper access controls keeps project data safe, available and shareable with the right people only.

### Devices in tough environments

Site conditions are hard on hardware. A sensible device strategy, ruggedised where needed, well-managed everywhere, and quickly replaceable, keeps people productive.
Mobile device management lets you secure and support devices wherever they are, and wipe them if they’re lost or stolen.

### Support that keeps pace

Construction doesn’t stop for IT tickets. Firms need responsive support that understands the pace of a programme and can help people on site, not just at head office.
That’s the model we run: fast, proactive support with cyber security and cloud built in, shaped around how construction teams actually work.
