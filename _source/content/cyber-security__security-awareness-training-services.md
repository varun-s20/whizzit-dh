### Your people are the first line of defence

Technical security controls have limitations. Most successful attacks begin with human interaction, a convincing phishing email, an unexpected phone call, or a genuine-looking login page. When someone believes a message is real, the strongest firewall in the world won’t help.
We help organisations reduce risk through practical training that addresses how employees actually work, using real examples rather than technical theory, so staff can recognise suspicious activity and know exactly what to do.
Training runs as an ongoing programme rather than a single session, with simulated phishing and reporting so you can see which teams need attention. Everything runs through the same UK service desk that handles your day-to-day tickets, so there is no separate team to chase. Across 2025 our average first response time was 9 minutes and our average resolution time was 6 hours, measured across every ticket we handled.
Common security risks we find
- Employees unable to recognise sophisticated phishing emails
- Passwords being reused across multiple business accounts
- Sensitive information shared without verifying the recipient
- Limited awareness of social engineering techniques
- Security incidents going unreported because staff are unsure what to do
- Inconsistent security practices across departments

### Helping a recruitment agency build a stronger security culture

The problem: employees faced increasingly convincing cyber threats. High email volumes made it difficult for staff to distinguish genuine messages from fraudulent ones. Although technical controls were in place, management recognised that improving employee awareness was essential to reducing human error.
Our action: we reviewed the agency’s daily working practices before delivering tailored awareness sessions covering phishing, password security, social engineering and secure handling of confidential information. Real examples helped employees understand how modern attacks work and how to respond safely.
The result: employees became more confident identifying suspicious emails, phishing reports increased by around 30% and management saw more consistent security behaviour across every department.
