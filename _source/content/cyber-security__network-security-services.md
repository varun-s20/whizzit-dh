### A secure network requires more than a firewall

Security vulnerabilities accumulate as organisations add offices, cloud services, remote workers and new devices. A firewall installed years ago, permissions granted for a one-off project, or infrastructure that was never replaced can all quietly widen your exposure.
Because everything continues working day to day, these weaknesses often go unnoticed. A network security review brings them into the open, so firewalls, access and infrastructure can be brought back in line with how your business actually operates.
Network protection is designed around your sites and how people connect to them, then monitored and maintained rather than installed and left. We are a team of five engineers supporting more than 30 UK businesses, from two-user teams to organisations of 150+ users. WhizzIT is Cyber Essentials certified and a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider, so our security fundamentals are independently assessed rather than simply claimed.
Common network security risks we find
- Firewall rules not reviewed for years
- Network devices with outdated firmware
- Excessive permissions between internal segments
- Remote access without appropriate security controls
- Limited visibility into connected devices and traffic
- Legacy infrastructure creating unnecessary exposure

### Network security FAQs

- [CTA] Isn’t a firewall enough to secure our network?
- [CTA] A firewall is essential, but it’s only the perimeter. Real security also depends on how internal access is controlled, how remote workers connect, whether devices and firmware are up to date, and how much visibility you have into traffic. We look across all of these, not just the firewall. -> /cyber-security/email-security-services/
- [CTA] What is network segmentation and why does it matter?
- [CTA] Segmentation separates your network into zones so systems can only reach what they genuinely need. It matters because it limits how far an intruder, or a piece of malware, can move if one device is compromised, containing a problem rather than letting it spread across the business. -> /cyber-security/dark-web-monitoring-services/
- [CTA] Can you secure remote and hybrid working?
- [CTA] Yes. We provide encrypted remote access with stronger authentication so your team can work securely from anywhere, without the connection becoming a weak point. It’s designed to protect the business while staying easy for staff to use. -> /cyber-security/security-awareness-training-services/
- [CTA] How often should firewall rules be reviewed?
- [CTA] At least annually, and after any significant change such as a new site, cloud service or major project. Rules tend to accumulate over time, so regular reviews stop old, unnecessary access quietly widening your exposure. -> /cyber-security/cyber-essentials-services/
- [CTA] Will a review disrupt our network?
- [CTA] No. Assessment work is carried out carefully and any changes are planned and agreed with you in advance, scheduled around your operations so your team and customers aren’t affected.Technology partners -> /cyber-security/configuration-review-services/
