Digital transformation

Simplifying digital transformation for Coventry businesses

Coventry is going through rapid technological change, with investment in full-fibre connectivity, 5G and smart city technology. For local businesses that means greater reliance on cloud systems, heightened cyber security risks and growing demands on scalability.

We help you navigate all of it. Our team combines instant remote fixes with dependable onsite support, so your technology keeps pace with your ambitions rather than holding you back.

Across 2025 our average first response time was 9 minutes and our average resolution time was 6 hours, measured across every ticket we handled. We are a team of five engineers supporting more than 30 businesses, from two-user teams to organisations of 150+ users. Most Coventry issues are resolved remotely by our UK service desk, and when something needs hands on hardware an engineer travels to you across Coventry and the West Midlands. WhizzIT is Cyber Essentials certified and a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider, so the security fundamentals are independently assessed rather than simply claimed.

Do you have an office in Coventry?

No. Our offices are at 124 City Rd in London and Caxton Point in Stevenage, and we would rather say so than list a virtual address in Coventry. The city is covered by our UK service desk plus field engineers who travel to customer sites. Most issues never need a visit: we respond the same working day and resolve most issues remotely. When a visit is requested we aim to have an engineer with you the same working day.

What does IT support in Coventry involve, and why is it important for my business?

IT support provides the maintenance, monitoring and continuous optimisation that keeps your systems running smoothly. It reduces downtime and resolves technical issues quickly, so your team can stay focused on the work that matters.

Why should I choose WhizzIT instead of other IT support providers?

We focus on proactive support rather than reactive problem-solving. Our emphasis is on prevention and improving system performance, and we keep everything aligned with your business goals rather than simply fixing faults as they arise.

Do you work with smaller teams?

Yes. We deliver scalable, cost-effective and flexible solutions, sized to fit your budget and growth plans.

Do you provide coverage across the Coventry area?

Yes. As well as Coventry itself, our services extend to the surrounding areas with the same fast, reliable assistance, both remotely and onsite.

What is the difference between IT support and managed IT services?

IT support tends to be reactive, fixing issues after they happen. Managed IT services are proactive, providing ongoing monitoring, maintenance, cyber security, updates and strategic planning to prevent problems before they occur.

How do I get started?

Contact us to book a consultation. We’ll discuss your current setup, challenges and goals, then develop a tailored support plan to get your systems optimised and running from day one.
