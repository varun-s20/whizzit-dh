Security-first IT

Security-first IT for Edinburgh businesses

Edinburgh is one of the largest hubs for tech companies in Scotland, and between 40 and 45% of businesses here suffer at least one cyber attack each year. That makes strong, always-on security essential, not optional.

We provide 24/7 threat monitoring and response, so threats are identified and neutralised before they impact your operations. Security is built into everything we do, from the service desk to the cloud.

Across 2025 our average first response time was 9 minutes and our average resolution time was 6 hours, measured across every ticket we handled. We are a team of five engineers supporting more than 30 businesses, from two-user teams to organisations of 150+ users. Most Edinburgh issues are resolved remotely by our UK service desk, and when something needs hands on hardware an engineer travels to you across Edinburgh and central Scotland. WhizzIT is Cyber Essentials certified and a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider, so the security fundamentals are independently assessed rather than simply claimed.

Do you have an office in Edinburgh?

No. Our offices are at 124 City Rd in London and Caxton Point in Stevenage, and we would rather say so than list a virtual address in Edinburgh. The city is covered by our UK service desk plus field engineers who travel to customer sites. Most issues never need a visit: we respond the same working day and resolve most issues remotely. When a visit is requested we aim to have an engineer with you the same working day.

What exactly is IT support in Edinburgh, and how does it benefit my business?

It’s a professional service that manages your technology, devices, networks, cyber security and cloud systems. Done well, it reduces downtime and improves productivity, so your team can focus on their work rather than their tech.

Why should I trust WhizzIT over other IT support providers?

We combine fast response, proactive monitoring and a business-first approach with transparent pricing and genuinely professional support. Security is built into everything we do rather than treated as an add-on.

Do you work with smaller teams?

Yes. We provide cost-effective, scalable solutions whatever your headcount, whether you need occasional support or a fully managed service.

Do you provide IT support across Edinburgh and surrounding areas?

Yes. We deliver both on-site and remote support across Edinburgh and the surrounding areas, so wherever you’re based you get the same responsive service.

What’s the difference between basic IT support and fully managed IT services?

Basic support fixes problems after they occur. Fully managed IT is proactive: it covers ongoing monitoring, maintenance and cyber security to prevent issues before they cause disruption.
